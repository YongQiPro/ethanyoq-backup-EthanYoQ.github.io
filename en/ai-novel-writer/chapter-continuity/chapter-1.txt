Chapter 1: The Ferry Receipt

Mara reached the Grayglass ferry desk at dusk on Tuesday. The clerk had already turned the sign to CLOSED, but he slid a damp receipt under the grille.

"Locker nine," he said. "Don't go there tonight. The archive opens at dawn on Wednesday."

He laid a brass key beside the receipt. A clean notch crossed its bow, right where a thumb would rest. Mara put the key in her coat pocket and left the receipt on the counter.

Ivo waited on the steps. He asked what the clerk had given her. Mara showed him her empty hands.

"A time," she said. "Nothing else."

They walked back without speaking. Halfway up the hill, the key caught in a loose thread inside her pocket. She eased it free and kept walking. For the rest of the climb she held it between two fingers, so it could not make a sound.
