Chapter 2: Locker Nine (Revised v1)

Mara waited outside the Grayglass archive on Wednesday. The windows were blue with the last of the night. When the attendant turned the sign to OPEN at dawn, Mara followed Ivo inside.

Ivo went ahead toward the records desk.

Mara took the brass key out of her coat pocket. She had carried it between two fingers the rest of the climb. It came out of the cloth now without a sound.

The lockers stood along the far wall. Mara found the number nine and fitted the key into the lock. It stopped halfway. She turned it over. The notch caught the light, then disappeared as the key slipped in.

Inside was a dry envelope with her name on it. The paper shook. Mara pressed it flat against the locker door, but her hand still shook. She wanted to open it before Ivo could ask whose writing it was.

The envelope was lighter than the trouble it had brought.
